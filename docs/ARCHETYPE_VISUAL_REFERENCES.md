# Archetype visual references

Approved visual morphology library for the 15 Living Morphologies archetypes. These are PLAN references. They are not wired into generation.

## Reference authority

`lib/catalog.ts` remains the authoritative architectural source: human-authored criteria, Low / Medium / High ratings, and Formal / Spatial / Atmospheric descriptors.

These images do not replace or override:

- the catalog
- Skill 1 architectural-to-Physarum translation
- frozen Skill 2 biological measurements
- frozen Skill 2 evaluation

They clarify how an authored intention may become visually recognizable in an abstract plan. Typology and archetype identity follow the catalog, not labels printed inside a board.

## What each image shows

**Physarum field character.** The yellow field is visual guidance for hierarchy: reinforced concentrations, strong primary paths, secondary paths, weak / capillary paths, and low-activity or abandoned territory. It is not exact simulation geometry and not a uniform web.

**Abstract architectural plan.** The white geometry is a simplified plan organization: flat, white on black, without trees, furniture, people, landscape, conventional walls and doors, perspective, or 3D extrusion. It communicates spatial organization, not a finished building.

## Non-template rule

Do not copy coordinates, outlines, proportions, branch counts, void shapes, node locations, symmetry, room footprints, widths, or angles. A valid generated plan may look substantially different and still express the same architectural logic. Geometry must still come from actual Physarum evidence.

The archetype sets the organizational grammar. Authored criteria and descriptors qualify that intention. Physarum sets variable geometry, hierarchy, irregularity, position, connectivity, voids, and reinforcement. The image is a visual audit of whether that relationship stays legible. Do not build 15 hard-coded plan generators.

## Plan-first direction

Skill 2 will eventually operate in XY plan space. Skill 3 will later continue a selected XY Physarum state through time and stack later states vertically: X/Y is the spatial field, iteration/time is Z. That change is not implemented here.

## Interface

These boards are not interface references. Do not imitate their layout, typography, annotations, legends, titles, or panel composition. The Living Morphologies application interface stays unchanged.
